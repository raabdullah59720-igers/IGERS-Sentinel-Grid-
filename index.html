Inventor attribution

IGERS-BD-01 is attributed to Abdullah Al Rafi, a Civil Engineering student at Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST).

Core vision
Recover useful energy from distributed infrastructure interactions, then condition, store and route it toward nearby loads.
Design philosophy
Technology-agnostic at the platform level, with each source requiring separate engineering feasibility analysis.
Positioning
A supplementary and distributed recovery concept, not a substitute for conventional generation.
