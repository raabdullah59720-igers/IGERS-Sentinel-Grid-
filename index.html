01 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

One integrated pathway from flow to useful energy.

IGERS-BD-01 is presented as a research and engineering concept: a coordinated architecture for capturing underutilized mechanical or hydraulic energy, converting it, storing it and monitoring the resulting system.
